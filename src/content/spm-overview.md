# Software Project Management — Study Guide

These notes cover two chapters of Software Project Management, based on *Information Technology Project Management* by Kathy Schwalbe:

1. **Chapter 9 — Project Resource Management.** How to plan, assign, level and lead the people and physical resources of a project.
2. **Chapter 10 — Project Communications Management.** How to decide who needs which information, how many communication paths exist, and which medium to use.

A final page has worked exam-style problems, a quiz and a study checklist.

> **How to study these chapters:** most exam questions are either *definitions* (Maslow, Tuckman, RACI, Covey), *scenarios* ("Which conflict mode is this?", "Which stage is the team in?"), or *small calculations* (communication channels, slack and resource leveling). Learn each list in order, then practise applying it to a short scenario.

## What each chapter covers

| Chapter | Main topics | Calculations |
|---|---|---|
| 9 — Resource Management | Six processes, Maslow's hierarchy, organizational structure, Covey's 7 habits, work assignment, RACI, slack & critical path, resource leveling, Tuckman model, conflict handling modes | Slack, daily resource totals, resource leveling |
| 10 — Communications Management | Internal/external and formal/informal communication, channels, communication management plan, choosing media | $\dfrac{N(N-1)}{2}$ communication channels |

## Key formulas and rules at a glance

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Communication channels | $\text{Channels} = \dfrac{N(N-1)}{2}$ |
| Slack (float) | Latest allowed finish − earliest possible finish. Tasks on the critical path have 0 slack |
| Critical path | The longest sequence of dependent tasks; it sets the project duration |
| Resource leveling | Delay **non-critical** tasks *within their slack* to stay under a resource limit |
| RACI | Many people can be **R**; exactly **one A** per task |
| Maslow | Lower needs must be met before higher needs motivate |
| Tuckman | Forming → Storming → Norming → Performing → Adjourning |
