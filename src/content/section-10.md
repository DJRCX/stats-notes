## Final advice

Don't try to memorize every formula before understanding what it calculates.

For every numerical topic, follow this pattern:

- Understand the definition.

- Learn when the formula is used.

- Understand every symbol in the formula.

- Follow one completely solved example.

- Solve a similar problem without looking at the solution.

- Practice a problem with slightly different numbers or conditions.

**My strongest recommendation is to give extra practice to Chapters 3, 4, 5 and 7.** These chapters contain several different calculation procedures, and simply memorizing the formulas will not be enough.
